Database Design & Management

I have developed, designed and maintained geospatial databases for projects in the forestry and agricultural domains, for internationational entities like the FAO, or private companies developing carbon projects in multiple countries with different needs in terms of collection, storage, monitoring and use of the data.

  • PostgreSQL/PostGIS: Built scalable geodatabases for REDD and ARR projects with project boundaries and attributes.
  • GeoNode Integration: Deployed collaborative spatial data platforms linked to PostgreSQL.
  • SQL + R: Used SQL queries to drive automated reports and spatial analysis workflows.

Activities:

  • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Understanding and documenting databases and variables
  • Extration transformation and load (ETL): Extracting data from different sources and transforming them in useful datasets in databases ready to use
  • Revision and mantainance: Some clients already have datasets that need some work, so I have developed processes to automatically clean up databases or incoming data
