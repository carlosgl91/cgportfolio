Data Collection & Tool Development

I develop tools to collect, preprocess, and manage environmental and spatial data, supporting reproducible workflows and efficient monitoring.

  • GEE Apps: Created interfaces for selecting and exporting satellite data by time, AOI, and cloud cover.
  • Python/R Scripts: Automated downloading, cleaning, and transformation of satellite and tabular data.
  • R Shiny Apps: Built interactive tools for land monitoring and NDVI analysis.
