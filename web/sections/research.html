AI for Ecosystem Conservation using Soundscapes

Project PINV01-530: "Soundscapes: Applications of Artificial Intelligence for the Conservation and Use of Ecosystems"

Institution: Moisés Bertoni Foundation
Funding: National Council of Science and Technology of Paraguay (CONACYT)
Role: Associate Researcher
Period: August 2024 - Present

Key activities and contributions:

  • Developing potential distribution models for various taxa using occurrence data, satellite imagery derivatives, climate models, landscape metrics, and acoustic data.
  • Programming geostatistical applications and tools (GEE, Python, R) for model automation and evaluation.
  • Applying and generating landscape ecology metrics, including spatially explicit layers for correlation with acoustic data.
  • Processing satellite imagery data for statistical analysis and modeling.
  • Delimiting high-priority conservation areas based on model-derived indicators.
  • Contributing to scientific paper writing.
